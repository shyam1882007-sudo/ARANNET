# Label Guide

Every message gets 3 labels.

## Label 1: Is it a scam?

scam = the message tries to trick or pressure the person.
Signs: fake prize, fake KYC or "account will be blocked", asks for OTP, PIN or card number, asks for a fee to release money or a parcel, fake police or government threat, unknown link.

legitimate = a normal, real message.
Examples: bank debit or credit alert, a genuine OTP with "do not share" warning, delivery update, bill receipt, school or family message.

### Examples
- scam: Your SBI account will be blocked tonight. Update KYC here: (link)
- scam: Rs.50,000 prize won! Pay Rs.499 fee to claim.
- legitimate: SBI: Rs.500 debited from account XX1234. Balance Rs.4,000.
- legitimate: 482913 is your OTP. Do not share it with anyone.

### Tricky cases (look-alikes)
- A real bank message that mentions OTP or KYC is legitimate if it does NOT ask me to click a link or share the OTP.
- A message with a link is not automatically a scam. Check what it asks for.
- If I cannot decide, I do not use the message.

## Label 2: Language
- English
- Tamil = written in Tamil letters
- Tanglish = Tamil written in English letters (example: Ungal account block aagum)
- Mixed = a mix, hard to say

## Label 3: Source
- real = I actually received it
- synthetic = written by template, by me, or with AI help

## Privacy rule
Remove names, phone numbers, account numbers and links before saving any real message. Replace them with XXXX.
